Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about gold purity, hallmarking, orders and delivery.

Gold purity indicates the percentage of pure gold in jewellery. Pure gold is 24 Karat (24K), but most jewellery is made using 22K or 18K because pure gold is very soft and not suitable for making durable ornaments.
Gold wastage refers to the small amount of gold lost during the jewellery manufacturing process. It is included in the final jewellery price and depends on the complexity of the design and craftsmanship.
BIS Hallmarking is a certification issued by the Bureau of Indian Standards that confirms the purity of gold jewellery. Hallmarked jewellery ensures that the gold meets government-approved purity standards.
Gold purity can be verified through BIS hallmark symbols on the jewellery. You can also check purity at authorized hallmarking centers or use gold testing machines available in reputed jewellery shops.
