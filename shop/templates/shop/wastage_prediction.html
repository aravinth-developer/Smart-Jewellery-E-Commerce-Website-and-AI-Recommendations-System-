📌 What is Jewellery Wastage?

Jewellery wastage represents the percentage of gold lost during the manufacturing process.

Formula:

Wastage % = (Gold Given – Final Weight) / Final Weight × 100

At Sree Selvi Jewellery, we calculate only the wastage percentage. Making charges and VA% (Value Addition) are NOT included in this system.
